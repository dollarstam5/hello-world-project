# Product vision

This is the canonical product vision document for the Vitala ecosystem.

## Summary

The platform is designed around trust, mutual aid, local coordination, and calm digital experiences. It aims to support people in practical, local, and meaningful interactions without creating surveillance-heavy or noisy UX patterns.
